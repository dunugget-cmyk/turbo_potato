FAQ
Questions? We've got answers.
Do I own my website? +
While you're subscribed, WebForge hosts and maintains your site. If you ever cancel, we'll provide you with all your content and assets so you can take it elsewhere. We believe in full transparency.
How long does setup take? +
Most Basic and Advanced sites go live within 3–7 business days after we receive your brief. Premium sites with e-commerce or complex integrations may take 7–14 days. We'll always give you a clear timeline upfront.
What if I want to cancel? +
Cancel anytime with no penalty. Just let us know before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees — ever.
Are edits and updates included? +
Yes! Basic plans include 2 revision requests per month. Advanced and Premium plans include unlimited revision requests. Just send us what you need changed and we'll handle it — usually within 24 hours.
Is hosting included in the price? +
Absolutely. Hosting, SSL security, uptime monitoring, and backups are all bundled into your subscription. You pay one flat rate and we handle everything behind the scenes.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan? +
Yes, you can switch plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle. Just reach out and we'll make it happen.
Will my site work on mobile? +
Every WebForge site is built mobile-first and tested across all major devices and screen sizes. Your customers will have a great experience whether they're on a phone, tablet, or desktop.
Do you offer e-commerce? +
E-commerce functionality is available on our Premium plan. We can set up product listings, a shopping cart, payment processing, and order management for your online store.
What do I need to provide to get started? +
Just fill out our brief with your business name, logo (if you have one), photos, copy/text, and any reference sites you like. No technical knowledge needed — we guide you through the whole process.

Send us a message and we'll get back to you within a few hours.
